## Executive Overview

Human Improvement (BuyHi) challenged the traditional whey and plant protein market with clean cricket protein—combining superior bioavailability with radically lower environmental impact. Leading the digital product and launch commerce strategy, the mandate was to craft a delightful, non-intimidating customer journey that educated buyers and converted initial curiosity into sticky subscription habits.

### Strategic Initiatives
- **Taste & Science Visual Storytelling**: Framed gut microbiome benefits and sustainability metrics through approachable, friendly micro-animations and typography.
- **Frictionless Subscription Architecture**: Engineered a seamless "Build-Your-Bundle" box customizer with one-click subscription management.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization**: Designed variant selectors, allergen transparency tooltips, and instant unboxing previews.

### Quantified Results
- Reached **62% subscription share** on initial orders, drastically outperforming the category average of 20-30%.
- Featured across leading wellness and climate-forward publications for benchmark brand aesthetics.
