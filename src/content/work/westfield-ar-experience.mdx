## Executive Overview

Westfield is one of the world's premier commercial retail developers. As physical retail adapted to changing digital behaviors, we launched an industry-first augmented reality and spatial computing platform deployed across flagship shopping centers, turning passive pedestrian traffic into high-engagement interactive discovery loops.

### Innovation Pillars
- **Camera-First Spatial Discovery**: Superimposed real-time wayfinding arrows, digital art installations, and merchant treasure hunts onto the physical mall concourse.
- **Merchant Incentive Protocol**: Connected spatial AR encounters directly to digital coupon wallets redeemable at physical retailer POS terminals.
- **Low-Latency Indoor Geolocation**: Integrated Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacon grids with camera VPS (Visual Positioning System) to overcome GPS blind spots inside multi-story concrete structures.

### Quantified Results
- Over **180,000 in-store coupons claimed** via spatial encounters.
- Documented a **+35% increase in visitor dwell time** across participating center zones.
- Won international retail innovation recognition for merging physical real estate with digital spatial entertainment.
