## Executive Overview

Smith Optics has set the standard for snow helmets, ChromaPop™ lenses, and technical mountain eyewear for decades. In transitioning direct-to-consumer digital channels to support athletic lifestyle consumers, we focused on precision lens education, helmet fit guides, and optical clarity demonstrations.

### Strategic Enhancements
- **ChromaPop™ Interactive Lens Simulator**: Enabled customers to preview visual contrast across snowy, cloudy, and bright sun environments directly in the browser.
- **Helmet & Goggle Integrated Fit Guide**: Reduced post-purchase return rates by pairing helmet shell geometry with optimal goggle frame sizes.
- **Streamlined Mobile Commerce**: Frictionless Apple Pay and Google Pay integration for on-the-go mountain gear ordering.
