## Executive Overview

Redefining the shopping center companion application from a static PDF map into an active daily utility. The unified Westfield Mobile App bridged physical real estate infrastructure—including automated license plate recognition (ALPR) parking gates, interactive multi-floor maps, and tenant sales—into a single high-polish iOS and Android experience.

### Key Capabilities Delivered
- **Ticketless Smart Parking**: Drivers register license plates and payment methods; parking arms lift automatically upon entry and exit with zero paper tickets or pay stations.
- **Dynamic 3D Directory**: Vector-rendered mall interiors with step-by-step navigation from your current position to any store, restroom, or dining venue.
- **Contextual Offer Feed**: Location-triggered member deals based on user preferences and center proximity.

### Quantified Results
- Reached **4.7★ rating** across more than 25,000 reviews.
- Scaled to **1.5M+ active users** across the flagship portfolio.
