## Executive Overview

GoalZero is the pioneer of portable power stations, rugged solar kits, and outdoor power resilience. As consumer demand for energy independence surged, the digital flagship required an overhaul to eliminate friction, scale across international distributor networks, and articulate high-technical product specs in simple, confidence-building customer language.

### Core Challenges
1. **Catalog Complexity**: Customers struggled to pair battery capacities with matching solar panels, inverters, and power needs.
2. **Checkout Friction**: Legacy monolith checkout was dropping high-AOV commercial and expedition orders.
3. **Omnichannel Fulfillment**: Integrating multi-warehouse logistics with real-time freight estimation for heavy batteries.

### Strategic Solutions
- **Interactive System Configurator**: Designed a dynamic power sizing wizard calculating household and expedition appliance runtime in real time.
- **Headless Commerce Transition**: Decoupled the presentation layer to deliver sub-second page loads across North America and Europe.
- **Story-Driven Product Detail Pages**: Rebuilt PDPs with rich comparison tables, runtime calculators, and video demonstrations.

### Quantified Results
- **+48%** lift in cart-to-purchase conversion rate.
- **-32%** reduction in pre-purchase customer service inquiry volume due to self-service sizing tools.
- **100%** uptime through Black Friday / Cyber Monday record sales volume.
