# Current favorite bike ride bar &#8211; Bonk Breaker&#8217;s Apple Pie

I still a big fan of real foods on the bike but when convenience is critical for fueling a 4+ hour ride up the PCH into the Malibu Hills you need a good bar with a nice sugar and FAT profile.

Bonk Breaker has a bar that embraces my current favorite fuel:

CHIA SEEDS.

Breakdown is:

7g Protein

34g Carbs

6g Fat
