# Wow. Coffee as an insecticide ?

[![](http://54.214.102.151/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/cbean-300x187.jpg)](http://54.214.102.151/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/cbean.jpg)

I have just heard a fantastic YouTube video (link below) regarding "Nutrition for better performance" by a strong scientist, Steven Wm. Fowkes,  in the area of "Smart Drugs" .

The overall video goes into a lot of the negatives of the current diets and negatively impacting foods.  However, at the 59min mark , a question is asked regarding "Coffee".  The response is that is :

"It's an insecticide" .  Mr. Fowkes then proceeds to ask how many attendees have vegetable gardens and use coffee grounds in their gardens to keep the bugs away.

WOW.

Take that #Starbucks.
