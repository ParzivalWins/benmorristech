# Great Bar &#8211; Easy find &#8211; Grocery Store

I found a simple bar that has a good nutrient profile.

Lara Bar - UBER bar. 

Great amount of FAT from Nuts and limited Carbs (20G) . Best of all , it's a natural bar without any preservatives and additives.

The taste is nutty and gooey but you don't feel hungry afterwards due to the high fat to balance the sugar/insulin response. 

Like the Bonk Breakers - My current favorite flavor is Apple.
