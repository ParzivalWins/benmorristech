# Importance of L-Carnitine .

The roll of L-Carnitine in an active individual or fitness enthusiast cannot be underestimated. As it turns out L-Carnitine assist with the intracellular transport of fatty acids from cell fluid into Mitochondria. It is this process where the Mitochondria receive their energy from Fat metabolism. Key point: 

This is different than sugar as fuel. 

L-Carnitine can be found in foods like: 

Red Meat

Nuts,

pumpkin, sesame and sunflower seeds

greens such asparagus and broccoli ![](http://img.ehowcdn.com/article-step-by-step/ehow/images/a04/gl/19/what-foods-contain-lcarnitine-800x800.jpg) RedMeat

 
"In the course of human aging, carnitine concentration in cells diminishes, affecting fatty acid metabolism in various tissues. "
