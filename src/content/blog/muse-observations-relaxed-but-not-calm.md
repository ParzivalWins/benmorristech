Recently, I learned an important piece of feedback which is critical to understand your mind/brain/body connection. 

There's a difference between: **Relaxed vs. Calm.** 

 ![](http://52.12.181.38/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Relaxed-NotCalm-576x1024.jpg) 

From this session, you can see that while my body (Heart Rate and Gyro/PPG) indicate a relaxed physical state, the brain/EEG shows a good amount of activity. 

I would attribute this to a ACTIVE mind with constantly bringing up PAST and FUTURE thinking. It indicates a lack of synchronization with BREATHING. 

So a Mismatch between Mind and Body which the breath provides the portal.
