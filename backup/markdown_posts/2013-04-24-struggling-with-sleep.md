One sample was :

Somnidren GH  ([http://www.millenniumsport.net/](http://www.millenniumsport.net/)) which claims to be:

"Sleep and Growth Hormone Potentiator"

In fact , I couldn't even find "Potentiator" in the dictionary.   Bad sign.

[![](http://54.214.102.151/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/Somngren-Sleep-300x225.jpg)](http://54.214.102.151/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/Somngren-Sleep.jpg)

While I have almost used Millenium's ZMA.  This supplement definitely had some ingredients that sent my body spinning.  One problem I have right off the top is an overabundance of any one substance especially a B-Vitamin. B-Vitamins are generally related to the energy systems of the body and the last thing I want is HIGH energy at the end of the day.  I want CALM and RECOVERY.

Now granted Ben from Ben Greenfield Fitness took this supplement in conjunction with Natural Calm.  I still had the opposite intended effect.
